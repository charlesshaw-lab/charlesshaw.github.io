Robotic Camera Arm

Conceptual Design — Team of 4 · Fall 2025
HERO IMAGE — Crane assembly render / drawing
Design Requirements
Designed for wedding photographers as a low-cost alternative to $100K+ industrial camera arms.
RequirementTarget
Reach≥ 1.2 m
Payload≥ 3 kg
End-effector speed≥ 2 m/s
Joint rotation speed≥ 180°/s
Degrees of freedom6
Cost< $8,000
Design & Iteration
Wrist-Elbow-Shoulder
01
Iterated 1A→1C, added DOF each round, still short of the 6-DOF target
Exoskeleton
02
Suspended-wire system reworked into a column/track design in 3B
Crane (selected)
03
Prismatic motion, fewest motors (3 vs. 5), highest score on decision matrix
Analysis & Validation
Objective
Confirm the design could meet motor torque, mass, and cost targets before a physical build.
Method
SolidWorks mass properties analysis; hand-calculated motor torque at full extension.
Result
Mass 45.9 kg (101.2 lb) — within tripod capacity; shoulder torque requirement 2.67 N·m; total cost $4,696.75 — under the $8,000 cap.
Integration & Outcome

Shoulder (rack-and-pinion + bevel gears), hip rotation (24V DC motor, 100:1 planetary), control arm, and DJI RS 4 Pro gimbal integrated onto a Mitchell Heavy-Duty tripod.

Conceptual design complete; not yet fabricated. Next: emergency stop + thermal management, then detailed mechanical analysis and physical testing.
